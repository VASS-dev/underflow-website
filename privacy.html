Privacy Policy

Effective: November 9, 2026

1. Who Is Responsible

Underflow is an independent project. The developer of Underflow is the controller of the personal data described below. For any question about your data, or to exercise any of the rights in section 8, write to hello@underflow.music — that address reaches a person, not a queue.

2. The Short Version

We collect as little as we can: an email address so you can have an account, and the technical minimum needed to keep the Service running and secure. Your music collection never leaves your computer. We do not sell your data, we do not advertise to you, and we do not profile you.

3. What We Collect

Email address — to create your account, sign you in, and send service notices. Password — stored only as a cryptographic hash, never in readable form. IP address and a device identifier — to keep accounts secure, recognise your devices, and detect abuse. Usage data — search counts and feature usage, used to keep the Service within its capacity and to understand what to improve. We do not ask for your real name, address, or payment details, because the Service is free and does not need them.

4. What Stays on Your Device

Your library — tracks, tags, wishlists, buy list, and settings — lives in a database file on your own computer. It is not uploaded to our servers and is not covered by this Policy, because we never see it. The application also uses local storage on your device for settings and your sign-in session.

5. Why We Are Allowed To

We process your data to perform the agreement between us — you cannot have an account without an email address. We rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the Service secure and available for the technical data in section 3. Where we ask for your consent, you can withdraw it at any time, and withdrawing it does not affect processing that already happened.

6. Who Else Sees It

Our hosting and database provider, which stores the data on our behalf and may not use it for anything else. Our email provider, to deliver account and verification messages. Google, only if you choose to sign in with Google, and then only your email address is exchanged. Website analytics are handled by Plausible, which uses no cookies and collects no personal data. That is the whole list. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone, and we share it with authorities only where the law genuinely requires it.

7. Where It Is Stored

Our server infrastructure runs in Amsterdam, in the European Union. If you sign in with Google, that exchange involves Google's own infrastructure, which may be outside the EU, under the safeguards Google applies to it.

8. Your Rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you and get a copy of it; have it corrected if it is wrong; have it deleted; object to or restrict how we use it; and take it elsewhere in a portable form. Write to hello@underflow.music and we will answer within 30 days. If you are unhappy with how we handled it, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live.

9. How We Protect It

All traffic runs over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are hashed with a modern algorithm and are never stored or logged in readable form. Access to production systems is restricted and authenticated, sign-in supports a second factor by email, and you can review and revoke your registered devices and sessions from inside the application.

10. How Long We Keep It

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. Delete your account and we erase the associated personal data within 30 days. Technical logs used for security are kept for a short period and then discarded.

11. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, tell us and we will remove it.

12. Changes to This Policy

If this Policy changes, the current version is always published at underflow.music/privacy.html, and we will tell you by email before any material change takes effect.

13. Contact

Underflow — hello@underflow.music
