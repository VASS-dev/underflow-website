01 · Search · Mass search

Search many artists in one go

Tick several artists, press Mass search, and their tracks from Bandcamp, Beatport, Decks and more arrive in one list. In this clip, four new artists give 557 tracks, starting from an empty database.

  • Batch search for up to 10 artists at a time.
  • Repeat searches are fast; the first scan of a new artist takes longer.

02 · Search · Five stores

Five stores, one list

Bandcamp, Decks.de, Beatport, Juno Records and Discogs side by side. A badge on every track shows where it was found, and one menu filters by store.

  • The Vinyl preset selects Decks.de, Juno Records and Discogs; Digital selects Bandcamp and Beatport.
  • Or tick any combination of the five stores.

03 · Search · Live activity

Watch every search as it happens

The Activity panel logs each search: which artist, how many tracks it found and how many of them are new. If a source did not answer, you can retry just that source.

  • One line per artist.
  • Fresh tracks are counted separately, so you see at a glance what changed since the last scan.

04 · Search · Labels

Labels, not only artists

Browse by label too: your favourite labels and their releases in one list, next to your artists.

  • Switch the left panel between Artists and Labels.
  • A label opens as a list of its releases, newest first.

05 · Discover · Related artists

Who remixed whom

Related artists with the relation type next to each name, built from the tracks themselves. Click a name to see that artist’s tracks and follow the scene.

  • C for collaborator, R for remixer, F for featuring, O for original.
  • Select an artist, press Find relations, and the panel on the right fills in.

06 · Discover · Grow your library

Add related artists in one click

Filter the related list to artists you do not have yet, tick the ones you like and add them to your library. Then search them, find their relations, and keep going.

  • Three views: all related artists, already in your list, or new.
  • Added artists join the left list and are ready for a mass search.

07 · Discover · Similar tracks

Find tracks that sound alike

Play a track, press the Similar button in the player, and a list of similar tracks opens in the right panel. Similar tracks come from cosine.club.

  • Works on whatever is playing right now.
  • Use the refresh button to ask again.

08 · Listen · Player

A player with a live spectrum

Previews play right in the app, with no new tab per release. The bar behind the controls is a live spectrum analyzer, and clicking it jumps to that point in the track.

  • Previous, next, play and pause, volume.
  • A button scrolls the list back to the track that is playing.

09 · Listen · Mini player

A mini player that stays on top

Switch to the mini player and keep listening while you work in other windows. It stays above everything else and keeps the main controls close.

  • Seek, previous and next, volume and play or pause.
  • Heart a track, add it to the buy list or tag it from the mini player.

10 · Organize · History

Remember what you have listened to

Underflow keeps your listening history. Open it to see what you played, or filter the list to listened tracks only.

  • Listening history in one click.
  • Filter to tracks you have already listened to.

11 · Organize · Date range

Filter by date

Pick a period on the calendar to see only what was released, or only what was added to your library, in that window.

  • Switch between release date and date added.
  • Handy for what is new since your last dig.

12 · Organize · Tags

Tags that fit how you dig

Create your own tag groups with colors: mood, use, key, genre, whatever fits your workflow. Tag tracks from the list and filter by them later.

  • Up to 7 groups and 15 tags in each group.
  • Filter with AND or OR logic.

13 · Organize · Sort and pin

Keep the artist list in order

Sort artists by name, latest mass search, latest relations, date added, track count or listened count. Pin the ones you follow to the top.

  • Show only artists that have not been searched yet.
  • Ascending or descending, your choice.

14 · Buy · Already owned

Know what you already own

Tracks matched to your local files get an OWNED badge with the file format, so you do not buy the same release twice.

  • Heart a track to keep it in Favorites.
  • Favorites show OWNED badges next to the tracks you already have.

15 · Buy · Buy list

A buy list, not a pile of tabs

Mark a track once and it waits on your buy list with its store badge. Review the list, then buy store by store.

  • One click filters the whole library down to the buy list.
  • Every track keeps its store badge, so you know where to buy it.

16 · Buy · Export

Take your tracks to a spreadsheet

Press the export button in the toolbar and save the list as a CSV file. Filter first by store, date or tags, then export exactly what you see.

  • Columns: artist, track artists, title, label, release date, added date, wishlist and buy list flags with their dates, downloaded, listened date and preview.
  • The file is semicolon-separated UTF-8, so Excel opens it with the right characters.
  • After saving, the app offers to open the file.

And more

  • Keyboard shortcuts

    Play and pause, previous and next, volume, search, panels, favorites and settings, all from the keyboard.

  • Import and export artists

    Load a list of artists from a text file, and export your artists and labels to files.

  • Label search

    Select labels and search every track on them, the same way you search artists.

  • Local library

    Add folders with your music and scan them. Matched files play instead of the preview and get the OWNED badge.

  • Several databases

    Create, import and switch between separate collections.

  • Open in the store

    Jump from any track to its page on Bandcamp, Beatport, Juno Records, Decks.de or Discogs.

  • YouTube fallback

    When a store has no preview, one click searches the track on YouTube.

  • Display options

    Compact mode, covers on or off, tracks per page, spectrum on or off.

  • Built-in tour

    The help button replays a short guided walk-through of the whole cycle.

Underflow searches Bandcamp, Juno Records, Beatport, Decks.de and Discogs. It does not search Traxsource or Volumo.
