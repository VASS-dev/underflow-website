Underflow / Features

What Underflow does

Short clips from the real app, no mockups. Each one shows a single thing you do while digging for music.

  • Mass search
  • Five stores
  • Related artists
  • Labels
  • Already owned
  • Buy list

01 · Mass search

Search many artists in one go

Tick several artists, press Mass search, and their tracks from Bandcamp, Beatport, Decks and more arrive in one list. In this clip, four new artists give 557 tracks, starting from an empty database.

  • Batch search for up to 10 artists at a time.
  • Repeat searches are fast; the first scan of a new artist takes longer.

02 · Five stores

Five stores, one list

Bandcamp, Decks.de, Beatport, Juno Records and Discogs side by side. A badge on every track shows where it was found, and one menu filters by store.

  • The Vinyl preset selects Decks.de, Juno Records and Discogs; Digital selects Bandcamp and Beatport.
  • Or tick any combination of the five stores.

03 · Related artists

Who remixed whom

Related artists with the relation type next to each name, built from the tracks themselves. Click a name to see that artist’s tracks and follow the scene.

  • C for collaborator, R for remixer, F for featuring, O for original.
  • Select an artist, press Find relations, and the panel on the right fills in.

04 · Labels

Labels, not only artists

Browse by label too: your favourite labels and their releases in one list, next to your artists.

  • Switch the left panel between Artists and Labels.
  • A label opens as a list of its releases, newest first.

05 · Already owned

Know what you already own

Tracks matched to your local files get an OWNED badge with the file format, so you do not buy the same release twice.

  • Heart a track to keep it in Favorites.
  • Favorites show OWNED badges next to the tracks you already have.

06 · Buy list

A buy list, not a pile of tabs

Mark a track once and it waits on your buy list with its store badge. Review the list, then buy store by store.

  • One click filters the whole library down to the buy list.
  • Every track keeps its store badge, so you know where to buy it.

Underflow searches Bandcamp, Juno Records, Beatport, Decks.de and Discogs. It does not search Traxsource or Volumo.
