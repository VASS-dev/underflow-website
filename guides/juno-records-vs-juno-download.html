Juno Records vs Juno Download: what is the difference

The names are almost the same and the closure news mixed them up. They are two different businesses, and only one of them closed.

Two companies, one history

Juno Download, the digital store, launched in 2006 and stopped trading on Monday 1 June 2026, without warning. Juno Records, the vinyl and DJ-gear shop at juno.co.uk, is a separate company. The two split in 2013, when the download side was sold to a US company, and Juno Records is still trading normally.

What this means in practice

  • Vinyl and gear from Juno Records are unaffected.
  • Digital downloads from Juno Download are gone as a store. According to Digital DJ Tips, people who already bought music can log in and re-download it, and support can restore an order that shows no download option when emailed the order number. Back everything up locally.
  • Carts and wishlists disappeared in their old form, though the last cart and wishlist can reportedly still be viewed as a link after logging in.

Where Juno pointed customers

The farewell message named Traxsource, Beatport, Mixupload and Volumo. For the full list with pros and cons, see Juno Download has closed: where to buy DJ music now.

If you still dig on Juno Records
