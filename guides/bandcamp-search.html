How to search Bandcamp by genre, tag, location and format

Bandcamp gives you two ways to find music: the search box and Discover. They solve different problems, and knowing which to use saves a lot of clicking. This guide is based on how both behave on the live site (checked November 2026).

The search box: good for a known name

Bandcamp's search page has tabs for all, artists & labels, albums, tracks and fans. Each result shows its type, title, artist, track count and running time, release date and tags.

It matches text. We searched for rhadoo and got his own releases (for example the album Dor Mit Oru from 2008) mixed with releases by other artists where his name appears in the title or tags: It Has Two Ends (Rhadoo Remix + Bonus) by Denis Kaznacheev, and Deeplin EP (including Rhadoo Remix) by FloatingChord. In practice, the search is only as good as the way a release was titled and tagged. See also how to find all remixes of an artist.

Discover: good for browsing a scene

Discover pages live at addresses like bandcamp.com/discover/techno. On that page we found:

  • a genre list (electronic, rock, metal, ambient, jazz and more) and, for the chosen tag, subgenres. For techno these include deep techno, acid, rave, minimal techno, dub techno, hard techno, house and tech house;
  • a category filter: all categories, digital, vinyl, compact discs, cassettes, t-shirts;
  • sort and region filters such as best-selling, fresh and artists from anywhere, plus a switch to artists and labels you follow.

Results load 24 at a time, with a View more results button.

Tips that actually help

  • Start from a subgenre, not a genre. "Electronic" is far too wide; "minimal techno" or "dub techno" gives a usable list.
  • Switch the category to vinyl or cassettes when you are hunting for physical releases.
  • Steal tags from releases you like. Result cards show tags such as city and country names (we saw Bucharest, Romania and Berlin on Romanian minimal releases). Searching those tags is a quick way to find the surrounding scene.
  • Use the region filter to find local labels, then check each label's page for its other releases.

Where the built-in search stops

DJ TechTools wrote in 2023 that finding new music outside a direct search had been difficult on the desktop site, calling Discover "a huge step in the right direction" while saying the platform "may have a ways to go". At the time they also noted that Bandcamp planned deeper searching with tag combinations. We have not verified whether combined-tag search exists today, so check the Discover page before relying on it.

The bigger limit for DJs is structural: Bandcamp searches Bandcamp. If you also buy from Beatport, Juno Records or Decks.de, you repeat the same search in each store, in a different interface each time.
