Traxsource vs Beatport: which store for which DJ

Both are download stores for DJs and both sell lossless files, but they are built differently. Here is what the DJ-press sources agree on, and where they only hint.

What each store is

Beatport is the broad one. Digital DJ Tips calls it the main store for electronic dance music, carrying most underground labels across a wide range of genres and sub-genres, with charts, a preview player, playlists, streaming and a browser-based DJ app.

Traxsource, founded in 2004, is the specialist. Digital DJ Tips describes it as mainly underground house, plus techno, progressive house, nu-disco and related genres, with store-compiled and DJ-compiled charts and an independent, anti-corporate feel. A record-pool comparison summarises it as best for house, disco and soul-adjacent music.

Formats

Traxsource sells AIFF, WAV and MP3. Beatport sells MP3 and lossless formats; comparison blogs list WAV and AIFF. Pricing differs by source and changes, so check both stores before buying instead of trusting an old table.

Beyond the catalogue

According to Digital DJ Tips, Traxsource is purely a download store, with no streaming or DJ-app features around it. Beatport adds streaming, a DJ app and other tools, which the article frames as an advantage for Beatport and a possible distraction compared with Traxsource's single-purpose store.

A simple way to choose

  • You play house, disco or soulful sets: start with Traxsource, then check Beatport for anything it misses.
  • You play across techno, drum & bass and other electronic styles: Beatport's breadth is the point.
  • You dig for one artist across everything: check both, since the same release is sometimes sold in only one of them.
