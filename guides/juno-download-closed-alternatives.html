Juno Download has closed: where to buy DJ music now

Juno Download stopped trading without warning. Here is what happened, what to do about the music you already bought, and what the realistic alternatives are.

What happened

Juno Download, which launched in 2006, stopped trading on Monday 1 June 2026. Its homepage said it had been a privilege to share music from some amazing artists and that the time had come to say goodbye. Its COO said streaming had become the dominant way people consume music, and that artists and labels now reach fans directly through social media and services like Bandcamp.

Juno Records is a different company

Juno Download and Juno Records split in 2013, when the download side was sold to a US company. Juno Records, the vinyl and DJ gear shop at juno.co.uk, is still trading. The closure only affects the digital store. Underflow searches Juno Records, so vinyl results keep working.

Re-downloading what you bought

According to Digital DJ Tips, past purchases remain accessible: log in and re-download. If an older order shows no download option, emailing support with the order number reportedly restores it within a couple of days. Carts and wishlists are gone in their old form, though the last cart and wishlist can still be viewed as a link after logging in. Back your purchases up locally while you can.

Where Juno pointed customers

The farewell message named four stores:

  • Beatport: the biggest dance store, with the newest releases and widest label coverage, but with higher prices, extra charges for lossless, slow performance and weak search.
  • Volumo: a smaller, independent store described as the closest in spirit to Juno. You buy a track and choose its format (MP3, WAV, AIFF, FLAC) at the same price, with free re-downloads and human-curated charts.
  • Traxsource and Mixupload: named in the farewell message.

Bandcamp is the other common answer. Purchases often go directly to artists and labels, but the interface is not made for bulk buying.

Do not rely on one store

The lesson of a store closing without notice is to keep your collection portable and your searching independent of any single shop.
