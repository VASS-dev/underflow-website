How to find all remixes by an artist across music stores

Searching an artist's name finds their own releases. Finding what they remixed for other people, or who remixed them, is harder, because stores credit remixers in several different ways.

A real example

We searched Bandcamp for rhadoo. Among his own releases came others where he appears as a remixer, credited in three different ways:

  • in the release title: It Has Two Ends (Rhadoo Remix + Bonus) by Denis Kaznacheev, and Deeplin EP (including Rhadoo Remix) by FloatingChord;
  • in the track title, spelled differently: Gabrielle (Pheroxone Rhadoo RMX), where it is "RMX", not "Remix";
  • in the artist credit: BQD018 Pherox _ Gabrielle by Pherox (Rhadoo, Beroshima RMX).

A search for "remix" alone may miss "RMX", "edit" or "rework". And a remixer credited only inside a release can appear under the wrong artist.

A method that works

  1. Search the name, not the word "remix". Scan the results for the name in titles and credits.
  2. Try the variants: remix, RMX, edit, rework, version, dub. Stores do not normalize them.
  3. Check each label's page for the releases you found. Labels often release remixes as separate EPs.
  4. Repeat in every store you buy from. The same remix can be a track on one store and part of a pack on another.
  5. Follow the connections. Remixers of an artist often remix for the same labels. Each new name is a new search.
