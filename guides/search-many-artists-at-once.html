Search ten artists at once: batch digging for DJs

Digging one artist at a time across five stores multiplies the same steps. A batch turns an evening of searching into one list you can scan.

1. Prepare a list first

Write down ten artists: three you play constantly, four from scenes you like and three that friends keep mentioning. A prepared list keeps you from wandering.

2. Search them together

In Underflow tick the artists and press Mass search. Their tracks from Bandcamp, Beatport, Decks.de, Juno Records and Discogs arrive in one list, up to 10 artists at a time.

3. Expect the first scan to take longer

A new artist takes longer to scan the first time. Repeat searches are fast, so batch the new names together and let them run while you do something else.

4. Review by store and by date

Filter the combined list by store when you want to buy somewhere specific, and by date range when you only want what is new since last time.

5. Turn results into next seeds

Related artists from the batch give you the next ten names. Repeat once or twice and the list builds itself.

Search all five stores at once

Underflow runs one batch search for up to 10 artists across five stores and shows everything in one list. Free during the open beta.
