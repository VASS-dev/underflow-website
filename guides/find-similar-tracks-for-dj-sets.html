How to find tracks that sound like the one you are playing

The best track for the next slot is rarely the one a store recommends. It is the one that shares tempo, key, texture and energy with what is playing now.

1. Decide what you mean by similar

For a set it is usually tempo and key first, then energy and texture. Two tracks can share a genre label and still clash, and two from different genres can blend perfectly.

2. Start from a track that already works

Pick a track from a set that went well. Everything you search for should answer one question: does this fit next to it? Starting from a strong anchor beats browsing a genre page.

3. Use the artist graph for scene neighbors

Remixers, collaborators and featured artists around your anchor are the closest names in the scene. Their catalogues often hold the same feel.

4. Ask for similar tracks while it plays

In Underflow, play a track and press the Similar button in the player. A list of similar tracks opens in the right panel, taken from cosine.club, and the refresh button asks again. It works on whatever is playing right now.

5. Audition in a batch, decide fast

Listen to the candidates one after another in one player and decide in the first half minute. Tag the keepers and move the sure ones to a buy list.
