What works

  • Post as a person who digs for music, not as an ad. Say what annoyed you and what changed.
  • Attach one short clip, not all of them. Mass search or Five stores is the clearest.
  • Answer every comment in the first hours. Ask what people would want next.
  • Read each community's rules before posting; several forbid self-promotion except in set threads.
  • Replace utm_source= with the place you post, for example utm_source=beatport-forum.
